JUST PROTECTED · PRODUCT BRAND LAUNCH CHECKLIST

Before you print the packaging,
check the name.

A practical first pass for founders preparing a product brand for Amazon. Save or print this checklist before you commit to labels, boxes, and inventory.

1. Write down the basics

  • Brand name, exactly as you intend to use it. Include relevant spelling variants.
  • Actual products: “stainless-steel water bottles” is more useful than “e-commerce”.
  • Country or territory where you need protection. A registration is not automatically worldwide.
  • Who will own the trademark: you or a business entity? Confirm the applicant before filing.

2. Keep different checks separate

  • A company registration, a domain, and a social handle do not establish trademark clearance.
  • Review the relevant trademark registers. Similar names can matter even when the spelling is different.
  • Consider relevant commercial use where applicable. Finding no register match does not prove a name is safe.
  • Classes help organize goods and services. Related products and well-known marks can matter across classes.

3. Check the Amazon requirements

  • Selling on Amazon and enrolling in Brand Registry are different steps.
  • Brand Registry requires an eligible registered or pending trademark and other requirements. Accepted offices and statuses depend on Amazon’s current rules.
  • Check the brand name on your products or packaging against the application and enrollment requirements before production.
  • Amazon decides enrollment. A trademark application or a legal-service provider cannot guarantee acceptance.

4. Before paying for a filing

  • Confirm the proposed owner, name, territory, and goods or services.
  • Ask what the quoted price includes: government fees, service fees, classes, and any additional work.
  • Understand what the preliminary review does and does not cover.
